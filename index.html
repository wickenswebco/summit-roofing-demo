07 / Good to know

YOUR
QUESTIONS.
ANSWERED.

How do I know if my roof needs attention? +

Missing or broken tiles, damp patches, visible leaks and damaged flashing can indicate a problem. A suitably qualified roofing professional should inspect the roof safely before recommending repairs.

Is a repair always better than replacement? +

Not necessarily. It depends on roof age, underlying structure, extent of damage and lifecycle costs. An assessment helps determine whether targeted repairs or replacement makes more sense.

What type of flat roofing is available? +

Common choices include EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass and bituminous systems. Material suitability depends on roof design, drainage, detailing and manufacturer requirements.

Can I request a real roofing quotation here? +

No. This is a fictional website made for the Wickens Web Co portfolio. The quote journey is an interactive demonstration. It collects no contact details and does not submit an enquiry.
