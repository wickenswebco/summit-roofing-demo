How do I know if my roof needs repairing?

Missing tiles, visible leaks or interior water stains can indicate problems. A qualified roofing professional can assess the condition and recommend appropriate work.

Will I need a full roof replacement?

Not always. The best option depends on the condition of the roof, the type of damage and the existing materials.

What roofing materials are available?

Common options include slate, concrete and clay tiles, along with specialist systems for flat roofs.
